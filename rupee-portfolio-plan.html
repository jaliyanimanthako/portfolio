Corrections and changes

  • Fix

    The T-bill discount isn't spare money. The cash left over after buying a bill at a discount is just uninvested principal. Don't spend it on gold. Roll it into the bill or keep it as your buffer.

  • Fix

    Keep the emergency fund in Singapore. S$4,500 covers about 2 months. Aim for about S$7k by end-2027 by saving half of the S$378 left over each month. From 2028, use the freed S$1,500 a month to bring it to S$10–12k. Coins in Colombo are not your emergency fund.

  • Fix

    Model T-bills after tax, at falling rates. Assume about 8.9% net now, sliding toward about 6.8% net if CBSL cuts rates. After tax and inflation the ladder barely grows in real terms. Its job is protection, not growth.

  • Decide

    Give the T-bill pot a purpose. For example: "boundary wall and house foundation on the Mirigama land, from 2032", or "6 months of living costs if I move home". A stated purpose tells you when you can touch it.

  • Decide

    Choose where the freed S$1,500/month goes from 2028. Suggested order: top up the Singapore buffer first. Then consider something you don't have yet, such as a low-cost global index fund. Then send more to Sri Lanka.

  • Keep

    No debt. Quarterly batching. Plain 22K coins only, never jewellery. T-bills rather than finance-company FDs. A joint "either or survivor" locker so family can deposit coins.
